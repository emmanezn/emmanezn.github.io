about the radio

Made by students.
Live on air.

Lab Rats Radio is our school’s student radio. It’s part of the XLAB course: every Friday from 3 to 11 pm, students take over studio 6A and go live.

01

One hour, one student

Every hour belongs to a different student, with their own music and their own vibe.

02

Same slot every week

The hosts stay the same, so your favourite is always on at the same time.

03

A poster for every show

Each student designs a poster based on what they play. That poster is the record you see here.
